The APK keeps your learning screens on your phone. Add your own HTTPS Orbit backend when it is ready. Never put AI or Supabase secret keys in the APK.

Use the base URL of a backend you trust. This setting stores the URL only—not API keys.

Orbit is an AI tutor that plans your path, then teaches it

Tell Orbit what you want to learn, how much you already know and how many minutes a day you can give it. It writes a learning path for exactly that - about 40 lessons, in the order that makes sense for you - and then teaches it one concept at a time.

What happens in a lesson

  • One concept, explained for your level - not a video to watch past.
  • A practice challenge on that same idea, which Orbit reads and corrects.
  • Wrong answers are scaffolded instead of marked, so the mistake gets explained rather than skipped.
  • A mastery gate before the next lesson unlocks, so nothing piles on top of a concept you have not got yet.
  • A doubt slot in the same chat: ask "why" in the middle of a topic, get an answer, and the question you were asked is still waiting for you.

Skills you start stay yours

Start a skill and it keeps its own lessons, notes and progress. On the free plan the monthly limit decides only when you may start a new name - never what you may revisit. Reopening a skill you already own does not cost you that month.

Your first 30 days are Pro

A new account gets the full Pro allowance for 30 days, without a card, so you can feel the difference between one skill a month and every skill you want. On day 31 nothing is locked away: the account returns to the free plan and keeps every skill, path and record it made.
