Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{effective_date}} · Operated by {{business_name}} at {{site_link}}

Orbit is a personal AI tutor. This policy explains exactly what we keep about you, why we keep it, who else sees it, and how to get it removed. We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising trackers on this site.

1. What we store

DataWhy it existsWhere it lives
Your email address and a hashed passwordTo sign you in and tie your progress and paid plan to youSupabase (authentication)
Learning profile: name you choose, skill, level, goal, minutes per dayTo teach at the right level instead of starting from zero every timeSupabase table learner_state
Progress: XP, streak, completed concepts, mastery scoresTo show your path and pick the next lessonSupabase table learner_state
Lesson conversations you have with the tutorThey are the lesson itself; the tutor needs them to continueSent to an AI provider to generate the reply; kept only for the request
Plan, order id, payment id, expiry dateTo know whether your paid plan is activeSupabase table learner_plan
A random device id in your browserTo apply the free plan's daily limit when you are not signed inYour own browser storage

We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN, CVV or net-banking password. Payment is handled entirely inside Razorpay's own window; we only receive back a payment id and whether the money was captured.

2. Who else processes your data

  • Supabase — our database and sign-in provider.
  • Razorpay — payment processing, subject to their own privacy policy.
  • AI providers (OpenRouter, which routes our lessons to DeepSeek, and also Google, OpenAI and Anthropic) — each lesson question you send is forwarded to one of them so it can generate a reply. Their use of that text is governed by their terms; we send only the current conversation and your level, never your password or payment data.
  • Render — the server that runs this app.

No data is sold, rented or shared for marketing. There is no third-party analytics or advertising script on this site.

3. Cookies

After you sign in we set two HTTP-only cookies (orbit_access, orbit_refresh) so your browser can prove who you are on the next request. They are not readable by page scripts, are not used for tracking, and are cleared when you sign out. If you use Orbit without an account, no cookie is set at all and your progress stays only in your own browser.

4. Security

Every learner's rows are protected by row-level security, so an account can read and write only its own data. API keys for AI and payments live only in server environment variables — never in the app, never in the Android APK. Production traffic runs over HTTPS and sign-in cookies are flagged HTTP-only, Secure and SameSite.

No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not reuse elsewhere and sign out on shared devices.

5. Retention and your choices

  • Download your data: ask us by email and we will send your learning profile, progress and plan records.
  • Correct it: edit your profile in the app, or tell us what is wrong.
  • Delete it: email {{support_email}} from your registered address and we will remove your account, your learning state and your plan row. Deleting a paid account does not create a refund outside the window in our refund policy.
  • Stop syncing: sign out and Orbit keeps working with progress stored only on that device.

Payment records that Indian law requires us to keep for accounting are retained for the statutory period even after account deletion; those records are not used for anything else.

6. Children

Orbit is meant for learners aged 16 and above. A learner under 18 should use it with a parent or guardian's consent, and a parent or guardian may request deletion of that learner's account at any time by emailing {{support_email}}.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date above and, for signed-in learners, show a note in the app. Continued use after an update means the new policy applies.

8. Grievance officer

Questions, corrections or complaints about your data: {{grievance_email}}. We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve within 15 days. Full contact details, including our registered address, are on the contact page.
