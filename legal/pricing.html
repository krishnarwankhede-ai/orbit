Common questions

Will it renew by itself?

No. Monthly and yearly are single payments. When the period ends you go back to the free plan and decide whether to buy again. See the cancellation and refund policy.

Can I get my money back?

Yes — full refund within 7 days of paying, no questions asked, and also if you were charged but the plan did not activate.

Do I need an account?

Yes, to start a skill — the account is free, and it is where your monthly skill allowance is counted so that clearing your browser cannot hand out extra skills. An account also keeps your roadmap and progress on any device, and holds a paid plan.

How do I pay?

Through Razorpay: UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and popular wallets. Orbit never sees or stores your card number or UPI PIN.

What happens if I hit a free limit?

The app tells you which limit you reached. Tutor requests reset tomorrow; the free skill allowance resets on the 1st of the month. Either way your progress is not lost, and the skills you already started keep working — you can keep studying them all month.
